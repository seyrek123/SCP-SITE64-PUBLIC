O5 COUNCIL REQUIREMENTS

01 // COUNCIL DEPARTURE

REQUIREMENTS TO LEAVE THE O5 COUNCIL

Council membership cannot be terminated through standard Foundation procedures.

  • Valid Council departure authorization
  • Council security review
  • Resolution of all active Council obligations
  • O5 Council approval
  • Submission of an official departure permit
02 // WARHEAD DEACTIVATION

REQUIREMENTS TO DISABLE WARHEADS

Warhead deactivation requires authorization from the O5 Council.

  • Valid O5 security clearance
  • Warhead identification
  • Target facility verification
  • Authorized deactivation protocol
  • External orbital satellite support
03 // DEPARTURE DOCUMENT

O5 COUNCIL DEPARTURE PERMIT

Personnel leaving the Council must submit a formal Council departure document.

  • Full identity information
  • Current Council position
  • Reason for departure
  • Final acknowledgement
