SITE-51

THE CARLSON BROTHERS

Before Site-51 became known as a Foundation facility, its history began with two young researchers: Eric Carlson and his younger sister, Sophia Carlson.

Their younger brother was suffering from cancer. Determined to help him, Eric and Sophia established a small laboratory in the basement of their mother's house.

Their earliest experiments were relatively simple. They studied cancer cells, cellular destruction, atomic structures and other experiments that did not require human subjects.

The laboratory was originally known as Carlson Brothers Cancer Research .

Because they were too young to legally establish the laboratory themselves, the facility was registered under their mother's name.

Their research progressed until their mother died unexpectedly. Without her support, Eric and Sophia faced severe financial difficulties.

Three months later, their younger brother died. Eric and Sophia were left with almost nothing.

One evening, while the two were resting on a bench, two men wearing black suits approached them.

"ARE YOU SOPHIA CARLSON AND ERIC CARLSON?"

The two men presented a classified document connected to an organization known only as the O5 Council.

Eric and Sophia discovered that their work had been monitored for a considerable amount of time.

They were offered access to a Foundation facility where their research could continue under the protection and resources of the SCP Foundation.

This marked the beginning of what would eventually become known as SITE-51.
